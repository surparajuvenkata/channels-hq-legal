Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Channels HQ is a private tool that its owner uses for their own YouTube channels. This policy explains what it accesses and why.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (upload and read access): to upload videos, captions and thumbnails to the channels whose owners signed in, and to read those channels' own video details.
  • Google Drive and Google Sheets (only files the tool creates): to keep records and assets for those channels. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to manage the signed-in owner's own channels.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's own computer and are never published or shared.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove Channels HQ's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
